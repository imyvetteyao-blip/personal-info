# Gilead Sciences — Job Shortlist for Business / Investment / Regulation / Research (non-analyst, non-AI)

**Prepared:** 2026-09-08
**Source:** Gilead careers portal (Workday) — `gilead.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/gileadcareers`, queried via the site's public jobs API. 364 live postings scanned.
**Your filters applied:** level = recent graduate / early career; location = no preference; background = strategy generalist + regulatory / legal / policy + science (non-PhD). Excluded: pure data-analyst roles, AI/ML roles, finance/FP&A/treasury roles, PhD-gated bench science.

---

## Key timing note

Gilead's **2027 summer internship cohort went live on 2026-09-08** (the day this was prepared). Almost every "Intern –" role below was **posted that day and closes 2026-10-16**. This is the single best-matched entry point for a recent graduate, and the window is ~5 weeks. Full-time Associate/Manager roles are posted year-round.

Eligibility for the internships: enrolled full-time in a Bachelor's, Master's, MBA, JD or PhD program at a US-accredited school, with continued enrollment in the fall after the internship; min GPA 2.8. (If you have already graduated and are not returning to school, target the full-time Associate/Manager roles in the "Grow-into" section instead, and watch for the rotational programs noted at the bottom.)
